AERONEXIS DYNAMICS - KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

PRODUCTION KPIs
Batch completion rate: percentage of batches reaching completed status vs planned.
Incident rate: number of incidents per batch. Target below 0.2 incidents per batch.
On-time delivery rate: percentage of orders delivered by the expected date. Target above 95%.

INVENTORY KPIs
Stock coverage: number of days of production possible with current raw material levels.
Reservation rate: percentage of stock reserved vs total available. Alert if above 80%.
Alert frequency: number of low-stock alerts per week. Target below 5 per week.

SALES KPIs
Revenue YTD: total confirmed revenue for the current year.
Average order value: total revenue divided by number of confirmed orders.
Customer concentration: percentage of revenue from top 3 customers. Risk if above 60%.
Order approval delay: time between draft and confirmed status. Target below 24 hours.

QUALITY KPIs
Critical incident ratio: critical incidents as percentage of total. Target below 10%.
Quarantine rate: percentage of batches entering quarantine. Target below 2%.
Open incident aging: incidents open more than 72 hours. Target zero.
