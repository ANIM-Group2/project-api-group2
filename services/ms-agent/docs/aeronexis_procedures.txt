AERONEXIS DYNAMICS - PRODUCTION PROCEDURES

INCIDENT MANAGEMENT
Any incident with severity critical or high must be reported to the production manager within 2 hours.
A batch with an open critical incident must be quarantined immediately.
Incidents with status open or investigating require daily follow-up.
Resolution notes must be added before closing an incident.

PRODUCTION ORDER PRIORITIES
Priority critical: must start within 24 hours of creation. Requires immediate resource allocation.
Priority high: must start within 48 hours. Supervisor approval required if delayed.
Priority normal: standard scheduling applies.
Priority low: can be scheduled according to capacity.

BATCH QUALITY CONTROL
A batch is at risk if quantity_produced is below 80% of the planned quantity.
Quarantined batches cannot be shipped and require quality review before release.
Completed batches must pass final inspection before being linked to customer orders.

STOCK AND INVENTORY
Raw material stock below safety threshold triggers automatic replenishment request.
Reserved materials cannot be used for other orders without logistics manager approval.
Stock adjustments above 100 units require admin validation.

CUSTOMER ORDERS
An order with status in_production means at least one active batch is running.
Urgent orders must be prioritized in production scheduling.
Orders with expected delivery within 7 days are considered time-critical.
A confirmed order that has not entered production within 48 hours requires escalation.
