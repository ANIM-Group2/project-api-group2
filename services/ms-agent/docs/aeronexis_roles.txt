AERONEXIS DYNAMICS - USER ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

OPERATOR (Karim Ait-Ouali) - Site: Toulouse
Manages production batches: creates, starts, and completes them.
Reports anomalies and incidents on batches he works on.
Can view manufacturing orders assigned to his site.
Cannot approve customer orders or modify stock levels directly.

LOGISTICS MANAGER (Claire Dupont) - Site: Toulouse
Manages raw material inventory and stock levels.
Creates material reservations for production orders.
Creates and tracks shipments for customer orders.
Monitors stock alerts and initiates replenishment.
Must approve any inter-order material transfer.

SALES MANAGER (Sophie Martin) - Site: Toulouse
Creates and approves customer orders.
Monitors order status and manages customer relationships.
Accesses sales analytics and revenue dashboards.
Cannot modify production schedules or inventory directly.

ADMIN / GENERAL MANAGER (Philippe Laurent) - General Management
Full access to all ERP modules across all sites.
Approves critical stock adjustments above 100 units.
Views executive dashboard: production KPIs, incidents, revenue, stock health.
The AI agent ARIA is available exclusively to the admin role.

PRODUCTION SITES
Lyon site: primary manufacturing site, handles standard aerospace components.
Toulouse site: secondary site, specializes in precision components and testing.
